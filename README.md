# MIRRORHIRE AI 360

## From Resume to Recruitment

### Overview

MIRRORHIRE AI 360 is an AI-powered career readiness platform that helps students and job seekers improve their interview performance through resume analysis, career guidance, communication assessment, and performance tracking.

---

## Core Features

* Resume Analysis
* Career GPS Recommendations
* Domain-Based Interview Questions
* Speech-to-Text Conversion
* Filler Word Detection
* Eye Contact Analysis
* Communication Scoring
* Interview Report Generation
* Interview History Tracking
